# Reporting & Auditing

This skill provides a structured approach to documenting security assessments and audits.

## Audit Classification Levels
- **Standard (Unclassified)**: Focuses on common vulnerabilities and industry standards (OWASP, CIS).
- **Enhanced (Secret)**: Includes detailed internal architectural analysis and sensitive system dependencies.
- **Critical (Top Secret)**: Comprehensive analysis of air-gapped systems, zero-day capabilities, and high-value target impact.

## Team-Based Audit Frameworks
- **Red Team Audit**: Focuses on the "can we get in?"—adversarial simulation, breach paths, and objective achievement.
- **Blue Team Audit**: Focuses on "can we stop them?"—detection efficacy, response times, and hardening gaps.
- **Purple Team Audit**: A collaborative loop of Red attacking and Blue detecting, iterating to maximize defense.

## Reporting Structure
1. **Executive Summary**: High-level risk assessment, business impact, and a "single-pane-of-glass" risk score.
2. **Technical Findings**: Detailed vulnerability descriptions, reproduction steps, and impact analysis.
3. **Remediation Roadmap**: Prioritized fixes based on risk (Critical $\rightarrow$ Low).
4. **Audit Trail**: Full log of tests performed, tools used, and timestamps.
