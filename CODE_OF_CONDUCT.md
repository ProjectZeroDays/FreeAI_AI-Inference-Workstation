# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Standards

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus on the technical merit of ideas and code.
- Gracefully accept and give feedback.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the maintainers via `security@freeai.local` (or GitHub private security advisory). All reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, contributions, or ban contributors who violate this code.
