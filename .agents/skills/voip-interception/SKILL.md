# VOIP Interception

This skill focuses on compromising voice-over-IP communications.

## Operational Workflow
1. **SIP Discovery**: Identify SIP servers and registered extensions on the target network.
2. **Interception**: Deploy a man-in-the-middle (MITM) or mirror port to capture RTP streams.
3. **Decryption**: Use recovered keys or exploit weak encryption to decrypt audio.
4. **Audio Analysis**: Use AI to transcribe and analyze call content for intelligence.
5. **Active Injection**: Inject pre-recorded or AI-generated audio into an active call.
