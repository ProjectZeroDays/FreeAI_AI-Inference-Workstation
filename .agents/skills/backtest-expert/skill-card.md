## Use Case: <br>
Developers, quantitative traders, and strategy researchers use this skill to design, test, stress-test, and validate systematic trading strategies before considering live implementation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users could mistake educational backtesting guidance for financial advice or approval to trade live capital. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as methodology guidance only and perform independent financial, risk, and compliance review before live trading. <br>
Risk: Backtest conclusions can be misleading when data quality, execution costs, slippage, market regimes, or sample size are not reviewed critically. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's stress-testing workflow, use conservative assumptions, and verify results across out-of-sample data and multiple market regimes. <br>
